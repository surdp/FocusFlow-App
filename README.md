# ⏱️ Focus Timer

The Focus Timer is designed for a single uninterrupted focus session.

You can choose your desired duration using:

- Hours
- Minutes
- Seconds

For example:

- 25 minutes
- 45 minutes
- 1 hour
- 2 hours 30 minutes
- 14 hours 15 minutes
- Any other duration supported by the application

FocusFlow does not impose an artificial three-hour or similar maximum on normal timer durations.

The timer calculates the selected hours, minutes, and seconds as the actual session duration.

---

# ▶️ Starting a Focus Session

After selecting your duration:

1. Start the timer.
2. FocusFlow begins the session.
3. The remaining time is displayed on the timer.
4. The progress indicator shows your progress through the session.
5. When the session reaches the end, the timer completes the session.

Depending on the platform, notifications and background behavior may continue to provide timer information while you are away from the timer screen.

---

# ⏯️ Timer Controls

During an active session, FocusFlow supports controls such as:

### Pause

Temporarily stop the active timer.

### Resume

Continue a paused session.

### Skip

Move past the current phase when supported by the active timer mode.

### End

End the current timer session.

These controls are intended to let you manage an active session without unnecessarily restarting it.

# 🔄 Intervals

Intervals are useful when you want to alternate between work and recovery periods.

For example:

```text
Work
  ↓
Break
  ↓
Work
  ↓
Break
  ↓
Work
```

You can configure the duration of the different phases and the number of cycles according to the available settings.

Intervals are useful for:

- Pomodoro-style sessions
- Study cycles
- Exercise/work recovery patterns
- Repeated focus blocks
- Structured work sessions

### Work duration

Choose the amount of time you want to work or focus.

### Break duration

Choose the amount of recovery time between work periods.

### Cycles

Configure how many work/recovery cycles should be performed.

FocusFlow then progresses through the configured sequence automatically.

---

# 📚 Study Plans

Study Plans are designed for structured study sessions.

Instead of running one uninterrupted focus session, a Study Plan can organize a sequence of study and break periods.

For example:

```text
Study
  ↓
Short Break
  ↓
Study
  ↓
Long Break
  ↓
Study
```

Study Plans are useful when you want a repeatable study routine rather than a single countdown.

They can help structure:

- Exam preparation
- Revision
- Reading
- Practice sessions
- Online learning
- Long study blocks

---

# 🎯 Daily Focus

Daily Focus lets you define how much focused time you want to achieve during the day.

You can configure your daily goal using:

### Minutes

Set a goal using minute increments.

### Hours

Set a goal using hour-based increments.

The daily goal is separate from the maximum duration of an individual Focus Timer session.

For example:

```text
Daily Focus Goal: 4 hours

Session 1: 90 minutes
Session 2: 60 minutes
Session 3: 90 minutes

Total: 4 hours
```

The Daily Focus setup currently supports goals up to 24 hours per day.

---

# 🔔 Timer Notifications

On supported platforms, FocusFlow can provide notifications while a timer is active.

Notifications can provide information about the active timer without requiring you to keep the FocusFlow timer screen open.

Supported notification actions include:

- **Pause**
- **Resume**
- **Skip**
- **End**

This allows you to control an active timer directly from the notification interface on supported devices.

Notification behavior depends on the operating system and the permissions granted to FocusFlow.

---

# 📱 Background and Lock-Screen Behavior

FocusFlow is designed to continue managing timer sessions when the application is not actively displayed, subject to platform capabilities.

However, Web, Android, and iOS do not provide identical background execution capabilities.

Depending on the platform:

- Timer information may continue through notifications.
- The application may need to be reopened to view detailed progress.
- Background execution can be affected by operating-system restrictions.
- Notification permissions may be required.
- Device battery-management settings may affect behavior.

FocusFlow therefore does not guarantee identical background behavior on every device.

# 🛡️ Focus Mode

Focus Mode is intended to provide a more focused environment during a session.

Its behavior depends on the platform.

Focus Mode may use operating-system capabilities, permissions, and platform-specific functionality where available.

### Important platform limitation

FocusFlow cannot guarantee system-level application blocking on every platform.

Android, iOS, and Web have different capabilities and restrictions.

Therefore:

- Some Focus Mode capabilities may only be available on supported platforms.
- Some capabilities require device permissions.
- Some functionality may depend on operating-system APIs.
- Web behavior can differ from native mobile behavior.

Focus Mode should therefore be understood as a productivity feature whose capabilities vary by platform rather than as a universal device-wide application blocker.
